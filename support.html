Frequently Asked Questions

Does Melody Map play music?

No. Melody Map helps you discover music. Songs are played using Apple Music or Spotify.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

Some Apple Music features require an active Apple Music subscription.

Is Spotify required?

No. Spotify is optional and can be connected at any time.

Where are my favorites stored?

Favorites and preferences are primarily stored locally on your device.

How do I restore Premium?

Open Premium and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I remove ads?

Premium subscribers enjoy an ad-free experience.
